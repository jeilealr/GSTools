Supplied compressed NumPy representation of the digitized Herten section S1:
array order [x, z], int8 shape (320, 140), scalar spacing 0.05 m. The full section
contains codes 0 through 9. The gallery reads section[:160, :] (8 m by 7 m local
cell extent) without resampling or category recoding. This crop has codes
1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9. Codes 0 and 6 are absent from the crop.
The original section-to-NPZ conversion procedure was not included with this
contribution; this representation was supplied rather than independently
reconstructed from the upstream files. No geological name-to-code mapping is
asserted. The NPZ source attribution string is preserved unchanged.
